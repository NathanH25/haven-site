Delete your Haven account

App: Haven (com.haven.campuslife)

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Haven and go to the Roommates tab.
  2. Tap Open on the Shared household card, then the People tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Haven account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email address
  • Your display name and profile
  • Your household memberships (if you were the last member, the household and its data are deleted too)
  • Messages you sent and your shares of shared expenses
  • People you blocked

What is kept

  • Expenses you paid for stay visible to your former household, shown as paid by "Former member", so their balances stay correct.
  • Reports about messages are kept for up to 90 days for safety review, then deleted.
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security.

Data on your phone

Your personal planner (budget, classes, reminders, shifts) is stored only on your phone and is not part of your account. Remove it with Theme → Your data & privacy → Erase all data, or by uninstalling Haven.
